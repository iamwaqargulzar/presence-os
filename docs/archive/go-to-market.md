# Go-to-Market

## Do not market the whole platform

Lead with:
“We make sure your business is properly represented, found and trusted online—and then continually work to turn that presence into customers.”

## Acquisition

Primary lead magnet:
**Free Presence Scan**

Presence OS should be its own first customer:
- lead discovery;
- presence analysis;
- competitor research;
- qualification;
- personalized outreach;
- CRM;
- follow-up;
- proposals.

## Initial vertical focus

Candidates:
- home services;
- dentists/clinics;
- professional/law;
- B2B services/consultants.

Create industry skill packs after validating repeatable value.

## Founding program

Use a small Founding Business cohort (e.g. first 5–10) to produce:
- Proof of Work;
- before/after evidence;
- onboarding insights;
- pricing/unit-economics evidence;
- referral/case-study opportunities with genuine consent.

Longer term:
Platform Owner → Partner/Agency → Customers → Locations
but do not build full white-label hierarchy before demand.
