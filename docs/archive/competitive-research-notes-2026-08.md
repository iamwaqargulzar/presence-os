# Competitive Research Notes — 2026-08-08

Dated reference intelligence; re-check before positioning or pricing.

## Yext Scout

As of May–July 2026, Yext positions Scout around AI/traditional visibility intelligence, competitive/local diagnostics, prioritized actions, verified Knowledge Graph data, and access through UI/API/MCP. This validates that AI visibility is becoming an execution layer rather than a standalone rank report.

References:
- https://www.yext.com/platform/scout
- https://www.yext.com/about/news-media/yext-opens-scout-visibility-intelligence-to-partners-with-the-launch-of-mcp-and-api

Presence implication:
Differentiate with broader identity/presence truth, natural agent UX, tenant-owned portable identity, and controlled multi-domain execution.

## Vendasta

Vendasta announced AI Search Specialist open beta on July 14, 2026, combining LLM brand visibility, onsite AEO/SEO and business-profile management.

Reference:
https://feedback.vendasta.com/changelog/now-in-beta-ai-search-specialist-your-clients-search-visibility-optimized/

Presence implication:
AEO/AI visibility alone is not enough differentiation; Presence must connect findings to canonical truth and cross-surface action.

## HighLevel

HighLevel's AI Employee offering includes Voice AI, Conversation AI, Reviews AI, Funnel/Website AI and Content AI, and its 2026 product includes voice-first Ask AI and natural-language workflow generation.

References:
- https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000003906
- https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000008022-how-to-use-ask-ai-voice-mode
- https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006100-workflow-ai-builder

Presence implication:
Voice and natural-language actions are market-validating, so differentiation must come from truth/authority/presence-wide intelligence and execution quality rather than voice alone.

## HubSpot

HubSpot moved Customer Agent and Prospecting Agent to outcome-based pricing in April 2026.

Reference:
https://www.hubspot.com/company-news/hubspots-customer-agent-and-prospecting-agent-now-you-pay-when-the-task-is-complete

Presence implication:
Instrument outcomes and attribution from the beginning, but do not launch complex outcome billing until measurement is credible.
